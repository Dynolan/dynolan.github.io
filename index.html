Sky Bet

My main responsibilities when working on Sky Bet projects included designing concepts for the daily sports offers, creating and adapting imagery for use in ongoing campaigns, static digital asset placements, print production, and template design.As part of my role, there was a strong emphasis on innovation. An example of how I achieved this was the creation of a series of cinemagraphs for use in email marketing campaigns. This resulted in doubling the click-through rate of these emails compared to those which used a static image.To assist other areas of the business with limited experience working within Adobe Photoshop when creating basic promotional assets, I suggested to the rest of the Creative team that we produce an internal design toolkit. Whilst this streamlined the creation of these assets, it also ensured consistency across the brand’s various channels. This included a series of PSD templates I designed with automated essential creative elements keeping all assets within the brand guidelines.In September 2019, I was recognised for my contribution to the team when I received an award for Brand and Creative Employee of the Month. This was partly due to my efforts in assisting with skills development by introducing design challenges to the team to consolidate the knowledge gained from training sessions. This included a monthly challenge in creating a piece of work using Maxon Cinema 4D, allowing more members of the studio to confidently assist with 3D work.

One of my responsibilities was designing the creative for various offers. I worked closely with the marketing and brand teams to fully understand the briefs while working to short deadlines.

I updated previous email creative with cinemagraph variants which resulted in double click-through rates compared with static images.

Another responsibility was rolling out campaigns. This entailed taking the campaign design and creating the assets required for all channels. Given the large number of placements and assets, I decided to create a roll-out template which meant that the repeat tasks required for each roll-out were automated.

I also created the adverts for the multiple weekly press bookings. Before I took responsibility for these, different images were used solely for these assets, which led to further production time being required. I updated the workflow to consist of one image set to be used per campaign. This meant that only one image needed to be produced to be used across all media.

I created a suite of editable Photoshop templates and actions for use across the business, keeping the brand consistent across the various channels and teams.

Brand & Creative Direction

Sky Betting & Gaming — Internal Design

I designed the logo for the yearly staff party, The Gathering. The organisers wanted to tie the look of the logo into the Sky Gaming brands. I suggested creating a 3D logo, drawing inspiration from the 3D constructs used on Sky Vegas while keeping the core features within the internal brand guidelines. This involved working within Adobe Illustrator to design the initial concept and using this as a base to work from in Cinema 4D.I lead the project and was responsible for keeping up the momentum by managing the time requirments with the studio planner and holding meetings with key stakeholders.

To create the initial design, I worked within Adobe Illustrator. This allowed me to import the shapes into Cinema 4D.

I used Maxon Cinema 4D to create the final logo. As using a 3D element was a first for an internal design project, I had to create a library of textures and materials for this design. These can also be used for any future 3D work.
